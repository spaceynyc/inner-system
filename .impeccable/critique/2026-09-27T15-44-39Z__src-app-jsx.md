## Overall Impression
A genuinely characterful object wrapped in a generic scroll-page. The individual frames are handsome, but the four layers (3D title, 3D watermark, HTML cards, fixed overlay) don't know about each other, so the two most common viewports show collisions, the ending covers its own exit, and the keyboard/screen-reader path is broken. Biggest opportunity: let the spectrum shape the chrome and make the play affordance honest.

## What's Working
- **One input vocabulary, many outputs.** Bass drives vertex wave + chromatic aberration + core emissive; highs drive roughness + shimmer; mids drive rotation and distortion. That coherence is the product's character.
- **The transcend card proves legibility is solvable**: 0.95 backing, 0.26 border, drop shadow, text shadow give 10.5:1 body contrast. The other cards simply weren't given it.
- **Loading and failure structure is above genre norm**: weighted progress, real dash-offset ring, WebGL boundary, context-loss recovery with an action.

## Priority Issues
1. **[P0] Bottom overlay collides with the final card and covers its only control.** Measured at 1440x900 the HUD (753,731,206x132) overlaps "↑ Return to the beginning" (764,724,330x36) and the activation button overlaps the card; at 390x844 the button covers the description and the HUD + "Enable Motion" cover the closing line and return button. *Why:* the peak-end moment is where the exit becomes unclickable. *Fix:* reserve the overlay's height in `.section-transcend`, make the HUD `pointer-events: none`, hide the HUD until active on mobile, fold "Enable Motion" into the same panel. `/impeccable polish`
2. **[P0] Hero title cropped to "IN / STE" on phones and clipped at 1440 px.** `FloatingText.jsx` uses fixed `fontSize={1}`/`maxWidth={10}`. *Fix:* size from `useThree().viewport.width`. `/impeccable adapt`
3. **[P1] Keyboard, screen-reader and reduced-motion paths are broken** (WCAG 1.3.1, 2.1.1, 2.2.2, 2.4.3, 4.1.2). First Tab lands on the invisible return button; no `<h1>`/landmark; canvas unlabelled; morph only via a global M key with no control or announcement; `prefers-reduced-motion` stops CSS keyframes only, the scene keeps moving. *Fix:* `inert` on faded sections, `<main>` + hidden `<h1>`, a real "Change form" button, `matchMedia` gate in the frame loops. `/impeccable polish`
4. **[P1] Play affordance is disguised and its feedback is false.** "INITIALIZE SIGNAL" with an orb never says sound; `playState` flips on click, so a blocked or buffering `play()` still shows "SIGNAL ACTIVE" and lit bars. *Fix:* speaker glyph and "Play sound" wording, derive active state from the audio element's play/pause/waiting events, reset on rejection. `/impeccable clarify`
5. **[P1] Contrast and typography defects** (WCAG 1.4.3): `.section-label` 3.7:1, `.activation-kicker` 4.0:1 at 9 px, `.section-description-fade` 2.7:1, `.preloader-text` 2.4:1 at its animated opacity, `.intro-tagline` 2.75:1 over the nebula, body copy 3.8:1 over the 6 % card; primary button in Arial. *Fix:* `font: inherit`, alphas ≥0.75 for labels, darker card backing as in transcend. `/impeccable polish`
